A small toolkit for the Bikram Sambat calendar — convert between BS and AD dates, and work out an age from either calendar. Built for dates from 2000–2090 BS (1943–2034 AD).

Bikram Sambat → English

Enter a Nepali (BS) date to see the matching AD date.

English → Bikram Sambat

Enter an AD date to see the matching Nepali (BS) date.

Both converters work independently — fill in either side, any time.
